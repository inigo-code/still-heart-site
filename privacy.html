Privacy

Still Heart has no accounts of its own, no analytics and no ads. Your files, settings and sign-ins stay on your computer.

On your computer

What you download, your history and your settings stay on your computer, in the folder beside the app and the download folder you choose.

When you sign in to a site

The site’s own page opens in a window. You type your password into the site, and Still Heart never sees it. It keeps the session the site gives back, in its data folder, and sends it only to that site. API keys are also kept there, encrypted with your Windows account. Sign out deletes them. Anyone with access to your Windows account and that folder could use a saved session, so don’t share the folder.

When it goes online

Still Heart connects to the sites whose links you paste, and to GitHub when you update gallery-dl. Those sites see an ordinary request from your computer.

This website

It’s hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. It sets no cookies of its own. Both services see an ordinary web request.
